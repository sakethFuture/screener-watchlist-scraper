Upload a mutual fund factsheet PDF

Runs entirely in your browser — the PDF is parsed locally and never leaves this page. Matches holdings against your live ledger (read from this browser's saved ledger data) and shows each matched stock's current Fundamental and Technical calls.

Works with any mutual fund factsheet — no fund-house-specific setup. Parsing is driven by matching against your own ledger, not a fixed layout, so results depend on how much a fund's holdings overlap your ledger.
How matching works: company names are normalized (suffixes like "Limited"/"Industries" stripped, spaced acronyms like "I R F C" collapsed, vowel-dropped abbreviations like "Fn" for "Finance" handled) and fuzzy-matched against your ledger's company list — not a fixed watchlist copy, so this always reflects whatever stocks are actually in your ledger right now.
Fundamental / Technical shown: pulled from your ledger's currently active quarter tab at the time you last saved it in this browser — open the ledger and switch quarters there if you want a different quarter reflected here.
AMC coverage: generic across fund houses — the parser doesn't hardcode any AMC's column layout or holdings-table header text. It reconstructs each page's reading order from raw text positions, treats any "text + trailing number" line as a candidate holding, and keeps only candidates that fuzzy-match a real company in your ledger. A handful of common report-section titles (performance tables, SIP tables, disclaimers) are skipped since those list fund names next to numbers too and can coincidentally match. Verified against three structurally different real factsheets (ITI, ABSL, Helios) with no per-document tuning.
